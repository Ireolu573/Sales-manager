Questions

Things shop owners ask first.

Not here? Ask us once you create your account.

Can I sell in bags, crates and kilograms?

Yes. Each product has its own unit, and you choose bag, crate, kilogram or head when you record the sale.

What if a customer pays only part of the amount?

Enter what was paid and AgriLedger records the rest as credit against that customer, with a due date you set.

How do reminders work?

Open a customer who owes you to generate a reminder with their name and balance. Add their phone number to send it through WhatsApp.

Does it work on a phone and a computer?

Yes. The phone app is for quick sales and checks. The computer view adds full tables and charts for the counter.

Can my staff use it too?

You can manage your shop profile and team from Settings, so you stay in control of who can record sales.
